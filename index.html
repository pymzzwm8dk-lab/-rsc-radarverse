CHAPTER 001 / THE SPARK

IT STARTED
WITH THE SCENE.

Before Radar Space Coterie became a platform, it was a vision conceived by Sope, better known as Trillz — a force in Abuja's nightlife, entertainment, events and artist management.

In the wake of Puerto Blanco's energy, a young creative entered his world through a mutual friend, Champion. Selling tickets at Veritas University soon became curating party videos, designing posters and managing social media. The work became a bond built on creativity and trust.

CHAPTER 002 / THE HANDOVER

A VISION
ENTRUSTED.

Trillz had an idea for a magazine: a platform to spotlight the music, creativity and culture they were privileged to be part of. Watching artists such as Odumodu and Blaqbonez bridge the underground and mainstream, he recognized a new wave rising.

With so much already on his plate, he entrusted that vision to someone who had shown what he could do. What began as an opportunity became a responsibility — and then something deeply personal.

CHAPTER 003 / THE FREQUENCY

MORE THAN
A BLOG.

RSC is a community built around shared music taste, discovery, stories and the culture we live. A place for the names, sounds and creatives that deserve to be seen before the rest of the world catches on.
